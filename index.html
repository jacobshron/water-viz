Every day, New Yorkers use

1,000,000,000

gallons of water.

That's about

1500

olympic-sized swimming pools...

or, about

1

Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis Reservoir.

This is the

largest

municipal water supply in the nation.

Rainwater and runoff are collected by two main watersheds...

...which are stored in 19 reservoirs and
3 controlled lakes across 3 reservoir systems...

...and transported downstate by a
system of underground aqueducts.

Reservoir Levels Over Time

This chart shows how reservoir
water levels fluctuate over time.

Large outlying reservoirs, like
Pepacton, Ashokan, and Cannonsville, tend to
be more susceptible to seasonal fluctuations.

Whereas central collection basins,
like Rondout, have more stable water levels.
